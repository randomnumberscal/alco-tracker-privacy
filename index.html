Alco Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Alco Tracker respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is stored and processed when you use the Alco Tracker mobile application.

📱

1. Data stored on your device

Alcohol records, drink amounts, notes, personal goals, streak information, reminder settings, language preferences, theme preferences and other application settings are primarily stored locally on your device.


Alco Tracker does not require you to create an account to use these features.

📢

2. Advertising — Google AdMob

The free version of Alco Tracker may display advertisements using Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK.


Google may process information such as your IP address, interactions with advertisements and the application, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics, security and fraud prevention purposes.


Advertising identifiers may also be used depending on your device settings, consent choices and applicable law.


Premium users do not see advertisements inside Alco Tracker.


More information is available in the Google Privacy Policy .

👑

3. Premium subscriptions — RevenueCat

Alco Tracker uses RevenueCat to manage and verify Premium subscription status.


RevenueCat may process technical device information and transaction information related to your subscription, including purchase information and Google Play purchase tokens.


Alco Tracker uses this information to determine whether Premium features should be active for the user.


More information is available in the RevenueCat Privacy Policy .

💳

4. Payments — Google Play

Subscriptions and purchases are processed through Google Play.


Alco Tracker does not receive or store your complete bank card or payment card details.


Google Play may process payment, billing, purchase and subscription information according to Google's policies.

🔔

5. Notifications and reminders

If you enable reminders, Alco Tracker may request permission from your device to send notifications.


Reminder settings and custom reminder text are stored locally on your device.


You can disable notifications at any time through Alco Tracker or your Android device settings.

📤

6. Data export

Alco Tracker includes an optional data export feature.


When you choose to export your information, the application creates a file containing selected locally stored Alco Tracker data.


Exporting, saving or sharing the file occurs only after you initiate the action and choose where the file should be saved or shared.

🔐

7. Security

Alco Tracker uses security features provided by the operating system and third-party services that use secure network connections where applicable.


However, no electronic storage or data transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

🗑️

8. Data retention and deletion

Data stored locally by Alco Tracker generally remains on your device until you delete individual records, clear the application's data, or uninstall the application.


Information processed by Google AdMob, Google Play or RevenueCat may be retained according to the privacy policies, retention practices and legal obligations of those services.

🔞

9. Children

Alco Tracker is intended for adults and is not designed for children.


Alco Tracker does not intentionally request personal information from children.

✉️

10. Privacy questions

If you have questions, concerns or requests regarding privacy or this Privacy Policy, please use the developer contact information provided on the official Alco Tracker Google Play listing.

📝

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Alco Tracker features, third-party services, technologies or legal requirements change.


When this policy is updated, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will also be changed.
