Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Alco Tracker respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the application collects, uses, stores, and shares information.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

Alco Tracker allows users to record information such as dates, alcoholic drink types, amounts consumed, personal notes, goals, streaks, achievements, reminder settings, themes, and language preferences.

This information is primarily stored locally on the user's device. Alco Tracker does not require a user account to use its core tracking features.

2. Advertising

The free version of Alco Tracker may display advertisements using Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may collect or process information including:

  • advertising identifiers;
  • device information;
  • IP address;
  • approximate location derived from network information;
  • advertising interaction information;
  • diagnostic information.

Premium users may use Alco Tracker without advertisements.

3. Premium Subscriptions and Purchases

Alco Tracker uses RevenueCat to manage Premium subscriptions and access to Premium features.

RevenueCat may process information including:

  • anonymous application user identifiers;
  • subscription status;
  • purchased product identifiers;
  • transaction information;
  • device and application information.

Alco Tracker does not directly collect or store payment card information. Payments for the Android version are processed through Google Play.

4. Notifications

Alco Tracker may use notifications to provide reminders selected by the user.

Notification permissions are requested through the device operating system and may be disabled by the user.

5. Data Export

Premium users may export locally stored Alco Tracker data to a file.

Exported files are created only at the user's request. The user chooses where the file is saved or which third-party application receives it.

6. Data Sharing

Alco Tracker does not sell users' personal information.

Certain information may be processed by third-party services used by the application, including:

  • Google AdMob — advertising;
  • Google Play — application distribution, subscriptions, and payments;
  • RevenueCat — subscription and entitlement management.

7. Data Retention

Information entered directly into Alco Tracker is generally stored locally on the user's device until the user deletes records, clears application data, or uninstalls the application.

Information processed by third-party services may be retained according to those providers' own policies.

8. Data Deletion

Alco Tracker currently does not require users to create an account.

Users may remove locally stored information by:

  • deleting records inside the application;
  • clearing Alco Tracker application data through Android settings;
  • uninstalling the application.

9. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information handled by the application.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children and Age Requirements

Alco Tracker is not designed for children.

The application relates to tracking alcohol consumption and is intended only for users who are legally permitted to consume alcohol under the laws applicable in their country or region.

Alco Tracker does not encourage alcohol consumption.

11. International Users

Privacy and data protection rights may vary depending on the user's country or region.

Users may have additional rights under applicable privacy legislation.

12. Refunds and Subscriptions

Premium subscriptions purchased through Google Play are subject to Google Play subscription, cancellation, and refund policies.

Cancelling a subscription does not necessarily result in a refund. Users may manage subscriptions or request eligible refunds through Google Play.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application, its features, third-party services, or applicable legal requirements change.

The "Last updated" date will be changed when significant updates are made.
